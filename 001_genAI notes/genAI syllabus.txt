Recommended Priority

If your goal is GenAI developer/interview preparation, prioritize:

LLM → Prompt Engineering → Embeddings → Vector DB → RAG → LangChain → FastAPI → Agents → LangGraph → MCP → Production GenAI

Don't spend too much time initially on the mathematical details of Transformer architecture. First become capable of building and explaining a complete GenAI application, then go deeper into internals.
